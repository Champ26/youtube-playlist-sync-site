Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Overview

YouTube Playlist Sync is a personal automation tool used to monitor selected YouTube playlists and maintain a personal archive in Google Sheets.

Information accessed

The application accesses information from the user's Google account through Google APIs. This may include YouTube playlist information, video information, and information in the user's Google Sheet used for the playlist archive.

How information is used

The information is used only to identify videos in selected YouTube playlists and maintain the user's personal Google Sheets archive.

Storage and sharing

The application does not sell or share Google user data with third parties. The application is designed for personal use.

Authentication credentials are stored securely for the purpose of allowing the automation to access the user's Google services.

Data retention

Playlist archive information is retained in the user's Google Sheet according to the user's own configuration and use of the application.

Contact

For questions about this application or this privacy policy, contact the developer through the GitHub repository associated with the application.
